Sling Panda – Privacy Policy

Developer: Sigil Solution · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sling Panda ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not have user accounts.

Your game progress (coins, gems, levels, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and by ad partners that AdMob works with, including:

PartnerPrivacy policy
Google AdMobpolicies.google.com/privacy

To show and measure ads, these partners may collect and process data such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings),
  • device type, operating system, language and screen size,
  • IP address and approximate (country/region) location,
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks).

This data is used to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad results, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to show personalised ads.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the game you are asked whether ad partners may use your data for personalised ads. You can change this at any time from Settings → Privacy in the game.
  • Advertising ID: on Android go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Reward ads are optional. You only watch them when you choose to.

4. Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by ad partners is kept under their own policies listed above.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/samiullah014/sling-panda-privacy/issues
